# About ALIS

How to cite ALIS, its licence, the support it has had, and how to get in touch.

## Citing ALIS

If you use ALIS in your research, please cite
[Cooke et al. (2014), ApJ, 781, 31](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/781/1/31), and
give the repository's address, <https://github.com/rcooke-ast/ALIS>.
