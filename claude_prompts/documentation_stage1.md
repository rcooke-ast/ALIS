## Design

### How the stage is built

- **The order.**
  - The build comes first (1.1), then the pages that it builds (1.2) and About ALIS
    (1.3). CI's job and the skill (1.4) come once there is a site to check.
  - `prepfit` is removed from ALIS (1.5). Then the GUI's texts take their name
    (1.6), from code that no longer has `prepfit`'s texts. The documents follow (1.7),
    for both changes at once, so that the code's batches are run on the code alone.
  - The review (1.8) and the close (1.9) end the stage.
- **One source.**
  - Each fact has one place: the licence's text is `LICENSE`, included on About
    ALIS; the version is `alis.__version__`, read by `conf.py`; the list of pages is
    `tests/test_docs.py`'s.
  - Where a fact must be written twice (the citation, on About ALIS and in
    `CITATION.cff`), a test checks that the two agree.
- **No page is written ahead of its stage.** A page that a later stage writes has its
  title, its sentence of purpose, and two comments: its sources, and the stage that
  writes it. Its stage removes the second comment when the page is written, and
  Stage 5's close checks that none is left.
- **A bug becomes a test first.** In this stage, the reworded texts of the dashboard
  keep their tests, changed with them.
